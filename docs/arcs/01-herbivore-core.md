# Arc 01: Minimal Herbivore Core

Status: closed through Rebuild Goal 027

## Purpose

Establish a transparent energetic animal that is stable under a controlled
reference, responds intelligibly to constrained food and activity, and exposes
a clean intake boundary for later forage coupling. This stage builds a useful
scientific core, not a complete macropod.

## Accepted Contract

The current core has:

- one physical body-energy reserve and one gut available-energy stock;
- a fixed 24-hour clock with hourly transitions and exact carried state;
- metabolizable food-energy conversion from dry matter;
- maintenance and separable prescribed routine activity;
- explicit feeding opportunity, bounded appetite target, and realized intake;
- one external non-depleting acquisition ceiling;
- derived reserve tissue and body mass with no dynamic feedback; and
- closed gut, body, and whole-system accounting.

Evidence clients additionally establish recurrent limitation, bounded feeding-
duration response, implicit patch order, known-route movement cost, variable
food-energy density, and repeated lower-quality exposure. These do not create
general production schedulers or spatial state.

## Completed Progression

1. **Conservation core:** body reserve, gut stock-flow, food-energy bridge, and
   repeated reference cycle.
2. **Daily ecology:** feeding windows, maintenance, activity, neutral reference,
   intake thresholds, and shortfall/repayment probes.
3. **Regulation:** reactive appetite, bounded sensitivity, repeated-day runner,
   acquisition ceiling, recurrence, and derived body mass.
4. **Static environment:** implicit patch exposure, carry-over, movement time
   and energy, and food-energy density.
5. **Integrated compensation:** appetite rate stabilizes lower-quality daily
   balance; duration reduces but does not erase its reserve offset.

## Canonical Reference Surfaces

Retain a small set of interpretable anchors:

- adequate-quality neutral reference;
- one prescribed shortfall followed by reactive recovery;
- one acquisition-limited day;
- repeated lower-quality fixed-duration exposure; and
- repeated lower-quality responsive-duration exposure.

They are diagnostic surfaces, not empirical parameterizations.

## Exit Criteria

This arc is complete because:

- the reference animal is stable and exactly accounted;
- gut delay and reserve dynamics are understandable;
- rate, duration, acquisition, activity, movement, and quality effects are
  separable and bounded;
- no unexplained drift or accounting pathology remains;
- known limitations are explicit rather than hidden; and
- realized dry-matter intake can be handed cleanly to a future forage stock.

## Deliberate Deferrals

Composition, protein, water, thermoregulation, digestive efficiency, physical
gut fill, multiple foods, learning, reproduction, survival, dynamic mass
feedback, and individual variation remain important later extensions. They are
not prerequisites for the first forage stock or homogeneous coupling exercise.

## Re-entry Rule

Reopen this arc only when a coupled result cannot be interpreted without a
specific missing animal process, or when Barry authorizes a distinct
physiological research question. Do not reopen it merely because greater
realism is possible.
