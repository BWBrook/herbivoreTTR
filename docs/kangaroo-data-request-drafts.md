## 1. Fowlers Gap

**To:** fowlersgap@unsw.edu.au

**Cc:** roosearch@protonmail.com; zooroo@bigpond.com

**Subject:** Historical Fowlers Gap kangaroo condition and pasture records

Dear Ingrid, David and Fowlers Gap team,

I'm an ecologist at the University of Tasmania, working on a mechanistic
model of how prolonged forage shortages affect herbivore condition and
recovery. Red kangaroos are our first empirical case.

I'm hoping to locate the records behind Moss and Croft's 1999 body-condition
study at Fowlers Gap (1988–92), and the 1994–97 field programme reported in
your 2025 Wild and Diversity papers. I noticed that the latter papers offer
original data summaries on request.

Our priority is dated body-condition or body-mass measurements, structural
measurements and reproductive status, matched to pasture biomass and
greenness and on-site rainfall. Where retained, animal identifiers and
repeat-capture dates would help distinguish changes within animals from
changes in the sampled population. We are interested particularly in the
timing of condition loss and recovery relative to changes in forage.

Could you advise who holds these records and whether they could be shared
for research? Existing spreadsheets, scans or seasonal summaries would all
be useful, together with the sampling methods and any conditions on use and
attribution. If the records are held elsewhere, a pointer to the appropriate
person would be much appreciated.

We would of course acknowledge the original data collectors, and would welcome
discussing co-authorship if the records contribute substantially.

Best wishes,

Barry Brook, Professor of Environmental Sustainability

University of Tasmania

## 2. Kinchega–Tandou institutional inquiry

**To:** info@environment.nsw.gov.au

**Cc:** EnquiriesTeam@csiro.au

**Subject:** Locating original Kinchega–Tandou kangaroo research records, 1980–86

Dear NPWS and CSIRO colleagues,

Could you please direct this inquiry to whoever looks after historical
wildlife-research records from the joint NPWS–CSIRO programme at Kinchega
National Park and Tandou in the early 1980s?

I'm an ecologist at the University of Tasmania, developing a mechanistic
model of herbivore condition during prolonged forage shortages and recovery.
We are trying to locate the original observations underlying the plant and
kangaroo-condition chapters in Kangaroos: Their Ecology and Management in
the Sheep Rangelands of Australia (Caughley, Shepherd and Short, eds., 1987).

The main records of interest are Shepherd's individual condition and
reproductive observations from 1982–84, and Robertson's dated pasture
measurements from approximately 1980–85, together with the associated
rainfall, site and sampling information. Any continuation through 1986 would
also be useful. The priority is to match animal condition to forage during
the 1982–83 drought and subsequent recovery; the published aerial counts
provide supporting context.

Do the original field sheets, data files or project reports survive within
either organisation or in a transferred archive? Even an archive reference
or the name of a former project custodian would help. If records are
available, I would welcome advice on research access, formats, attribution
and any conditions on reuse.

We would of course acknowledge the original data collectors, and would welcome
discussing co-authorship if the records contribute substantially.

Many thanks,

Barry Brook, Professor of Environmental Sustainability

University of Tasmania
